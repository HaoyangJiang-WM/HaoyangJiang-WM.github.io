03 / EQUATIONS BEFORE LABELS

One integral.
A learned history dependence.

The retained NIEs use source-state Volterra kernels. They are not the target-state attention model and are not official ANIE or Spectral NIE reproductions.

y^(t)=gθ,h(t)+∫0tGθ(t−s,y^(s),b(s))ds

h: supplied sensor history. b: known control. G: learned source interaction and time kernel. The future trajectory inside the integral is unknown and solved self-consistently.

What is the history term?

g is computed from the same kernel, not an extra prediction network:

gθ,h(t) = h(0) + ∫−H0 [Gθ(t−s, h(s), b(s)) − Gθ(−s, h(s), b(s))] ds.

This anchors the equation at the exact current state while retaining the complete supplied history. No future labels enter the solve.

Neural DDE

ẏ = Fθ(y(t), y(t−τ₁), …, b)

Four delay locations and all response-network weights are learned. Delayed future values come from the model’s own prediction history.

Diffusion and waves use a learned time MLP. Relaxation uses a constant-plus-positive-decay family; rates and mixtures are learned, not supplied from the generator.

Selection and data

Six systems, four NIE variants and strong history baselines were screened. Three candidates were locked before fresh confirmation B. Each case has 64 same-family and 64 frequency-shift inputs. Training uses 128 histories, validation 32, 0.5% observation noise, exact current state and the same known controls.

Numerical and cost checks

All confirmation models were re-evaluated at half future time step. Large NIE vs history-NODE gaps remained; wave NIE vs DDE ordering changed. A 45 single-thread CPU-second threshold was used per training configuration; actual durations and all failed exploratory solves remain in the evidence.

What is not claimed?

No measured engineering data, full-field reconstruction, universal NIE superiority or state-of-the-art result. Neural DDE is better on same-family diffusion and relaxation. Wave frequency transfer fails. More NIE variants than NODE variants were screened; the model-search budgets were not equal.
