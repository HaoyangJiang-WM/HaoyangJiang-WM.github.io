03 / NEURAL EQUATIONS

One integral.
Learned history dependence.

y^(t)=gθ,h(t)+∫0tGθ(t−s,y^(s),b(s))ds

h: sensor history · b: boundary input · G: learned kernel. The future trajectory is solved self-consistently.

History term and implementation
gθ,h(t) = h(0) + ∫−H0 [Gθ(t−s, h(s), b(s)) − Gθ(−s, h(s), b(s))] ds.

The same kernel supplies the history term and enforces the initial value. All weights are trained jointly. These are source-state Volterra models, not reproductions of the full ANIE architecture.

Neural ODE

ż = fθ(z, b)
z(0) = Eθ(h)

Encode the history into a learned state, then integrate its vector field.

RK4 · discrete adjoints

Neural DDE

Learn the delay locations and how historical states influence the response.

Evaluation details & reproducibility

128 training histories, 32 validation histories, 0.5% observation noise, and shared controls. Parameters are learned end to end. Each configuration has a 45-second single-thread CPU training threshold. Full protocol, benchmark coverage, failures, and numerical refinement are documented below.

Replay curves use temporal subsampling and 16-bit display quantization; statistics use the original evaluations. DDE is more accurate on same-family diffusion and relaxation. Wave frequency transfer has high error. Results describe these simulations and implementations, not a universal ranking.
