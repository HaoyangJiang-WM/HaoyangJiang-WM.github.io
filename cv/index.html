Research Profile

I develop machine learning methods for physical dynamical systems, with a background in geophysics. My work spans generative models, neural operators, and graph learning for partial differential equations and inverse problems. I combine variational modeling, numerical simulation, and optimization to study stability and generalization, with applications to differentiable wave inversion, data assimilation, and uncertainty propagation.

Selected Research Contributions

  • Variational Generative Modeling

    Derived terminal velocities, transport costs, and training targets from minimum-acceleration principles for second-order flow matching. Evaluated the approach on physical fields, images, and video. VTV-FM code

  • Physics-Guided Graph Learning

    Developed boundary proxies and physics refiners for open dynamical systems. Used direction-aware operators and shallow-water simulations to study transport accuracy and long-horizon forecast stability. OpenBoundary code

  • Neural Operators for PDE Inference

    Developed Fredholm-integral and transfer-operator methods for boundary-value problems, with reusable forward and inverse solution maps that account for boundary structure.

  • Inverse Problems and Uncertainty

    Built differentiable full-waveform inversion workflows that combine wave physics, learned priors, and frequency continuation. Studied how uncertainty in atmospheric density affects orbital predictions. FWI code
